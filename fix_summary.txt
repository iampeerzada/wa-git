The error you are seeing in your screenshot (`Invalid parameter: config_id is required` / `It looks like this app isn't available`) is happening for two reasons:

1. **The Facebook JS SDK is generating bad URLs:** In your screenshot, the URL starts with `v20.0` and uses `app_id` instead of the standard OAuth `client_id`. This happens because the Facebook SDK caches old settings and fails to update properly.
2. **Meta App Dashboard Configuration:** When Facebook says "config_id is required" (even when it is clearly in the URL), it usually means your Facebook App is set up with the standard **"Facebook Login"** product, rather than the required **"Facebook Login for Business"** product. Facebook ignores the `config_id` if the correct business product isn't active.

I have **deleted the Facebook SDK completely** from the `Connect with Facebook` button. Instead, I wrote a custom, fail-safe popup window that generates the exact, pristine `v26.0` OAuth URL required by Meta:
`https://www.facebook.com/v26.0/dialog/oauth?client_id=...&redirect_uri=...&response_type=code&config_id=...`

This custom popup listens for Facebook to redirect back to your site, securely captures the `code`, and completes the backend exchange. No more `v20.0`, no more `FB.login` cache bugs, and no more `response_type=token` errors.
