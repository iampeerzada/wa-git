### 3. How to fetch WABA ID and Phone Number ID manually?
Since we are using the hardcoded URL, the popup will not magically auto-fill the fields on your website when it finishes. Here is how you manually get them to provision the instance:
1. Complete the steps in the Facebook popup window (create your WhatsApp account, etc).
2. Go to your **Meta App Dashboard** (developers.facebook.com) -> click your app.
3. In the left menu, scroll down to **WhatsApp** -> **API Setup**.
4. Right there on the screen, you will see your **Phone Number ID** and **WhatsApp Business Account ID**. Copy those.
5. In that same screen, generate an **Access Token** (Temporary or System User Token).
6. Go back to your website, and paste all three of those into the manual entry fields just below the "Connect with Facebook" button and click **Provision Instance**.

**Final Step:** Please run `git pull` on your production server, then `npm run build`, and finally restart your backend Node processes (`pm2 restart all` or similar) so the backend actually picks up the `v26.0` changes in `queue-worker.js` and `server.cjs`!
